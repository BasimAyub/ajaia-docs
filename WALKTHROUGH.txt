Recommended reviewer flow:
1. Select Daniel, create a document, format it, wait for Saved, then reload.
2. Share Q3 Product Strategy with Maya as a viewer.
3. Select Maya and confirm the document appears under Shared with me as read-only.
4. Select Daniel, edit Customer Research Synthesis, save, and reload.
5. Import a .txt or .md file under 250 KB and confirm it opens as an editable document.

Intentional limitation note:
This review build uses seeded demo identities instead of real authentication. Authorization is enforced server-side for owner, editor, viewer, and unrelated-user access. Real-time collaboration, comments, history, .docx import, and offline sync are intentionally out of scope.
